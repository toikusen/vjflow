# Hold events round 3 (Q2), 2026-11-09

This round added a sixth hold event, made every new ink on a line pass the same 4.5:1 test in plan and renderer, and merged the work into feat/lyric-editing. Coverage of 1.5–3 s lines is still far below the 80 % aim.

Merged from feat/rhythm-gaps (92f4bce, 82d566e).

## What changed in r3

- **A sixth hold event, `line-ink-settle`.** When no other family fits a world line, every character takes a second ink at once and keeps it. Position, box, tracking and opacity do not change. A plain world stays one ink across the whole line.
- **Fewer hold candidates.**
  - `crack` and `plate-slip` are out of the hold pool.
  - Pulses (go out and come back) are out for lines under 3 s.
- **One ink test, shared by plan and renderer** (`render/hold-room.ts`):
  - `groundsUnder` lists every ground under the words: the carrier, else background + measured under + plate + ground.
  - `keyFor` is the key ink.
  - `inkChecks` covers key pulses, phrase swap and the second ink. Each new ink reads at 4.5:1 on every ground.
  - New inks are only laid in a line's first set and never before a later cut.
- **One show time, shared by plan and renderer** (`render/show-time.ts`). A hold ends HOLD_BEFORE_NEXT before whichever comes first:
  - the next line shows;
  - a handoff into the next line starts;
  - this line's Q4 linked exit starts its LINK_PREPARE.
- **Records in the plan.** Each hold's duration is stored in the plan. Each line without one records its skip reason, the families its empty pool fell for (`beatWhy`: span / palette / carrier / grow / world-policy), and whether it had a safe window (`beatSafe`).
- **The light lock keeps every cut.** Only the change of light gives way. Plan sections equal base (21 084).
- **Measurement fix.** `hold_measure.py` subtracts the pre-event difference on both the on/off path and the ink path.

## What remains

- **Measure the real ground at every cut.** The renderer measures what is under a line once, at the line's middle. The plan sees only the palette. So inks are kept out of anything after a cut or a change of set. That rule, together with key inks that fail 4.5:1 on the page's plate or ground, costs most of the 1.5–3 s coverage.
- **Recovering those lines** needs the ground under the words measured at each cut (per section `under`), checked by the same `inkChecks`.
- **Carrier-settle and panel-open** did not appear in the clip windows. They need a world with real carriers to be judged on frames.
