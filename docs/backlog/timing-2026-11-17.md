# Line timing — backlog after the 2026-11-17 review rounds

The corpus timing audit (`scripts/lyric-vj/timing-audit.mts`) passes on feat/video-tier. These are the known leftovers
the reviews raised and this round did not fix, with where to look.

1. **A held line may run past its shot's planned end.** A stack/couplet line stays up to its floor (last character + one
   character's time) even where its shot's picture has ended, so its words can stand over the next shot's graphics or
   subject, in the ink chosen for the previous ground.
   - `src/app/lyric-vj/render/engine.ts:1274` (`shotOver`: `Math.max(shot.end, floorOf(...) + EXIT_LEAD)`)
   - Fix direction: extend the held shot's picture with its line, or re-pick the line's ink on the ground it ends on.

2. **Audit rule (2) exemptions are coarse.** Two lines up at once are allowed when they share a shot whose earlier line
   is `exempt` (held), when either is a shout, or when the second is carried in by a handoff. Not checked: a couplet's
   line against the line two after it (i+2), a shout against its host's neighbours, and the length of a handoff's window.
   - `scripts/lyric-vj/timing-audit.mts:60-62`

3. **World-event corpus diversity.** Only the synthetic duet/same-start jobs and the free-path clips carry world events;
   the per-line naming (`src/app/lyric-vj/render/world-named.ts:8`) is exercised on few real songs.
   - Add saved world-event jobs of more worlds (chalkboard, document, notebook on 雙截棍, 三年後的大雪) to
     `scripts/lyric-vj/timing-audit.jobs.txt`.

4. **PLAN_TAIL is about one frame.** A line a world event acts on is read at the composer's tail, 0.04 s past its last sung
   character (`src/app/lyric-vj/render/sung-tail.ts:80`, applied at `engine.ts:1194`), not one character's time (1/12 s).
   With a word or sung reveal the last word can show for little more than a frame; about two real lines in the corpus are
   at that edge. Pairing at 1/12 s in the composer (`lineplan-core.ts` planPairs) re-planned fast rap past the world-event
   scheduler's own checks (3b, 4a, 4c, pairs-rules on 雙截棍); fixing that needs the scheduler to plan shared shots.

5. **Unmerged lines under REST_FLOOR.** Rule (5) of the audit holds lines set together to the reading floor; other lines'
   sizes are their templates' (an orbit, a quote's call). The audit counts them apart
   (`scripts/lyric-vj/timing-audit.mts:133`, "unmerged lines under REST_FLOOR").

6. **A seated pair with a third voice in the next shot.** Where two lines already share a stack shot (seated apart) and
   the first is still sung into the next shot's line, nothing sets that third voice together: the held line leaves when
   the next shot's line shows and the overlap is logged (the readable gate fails it). Seen with 天文対話 c008 sung on
   past c009 into c010 (a synthetic of `scripts/lyric-vj/timing-audit-synth.mts`, not in the corpus).
   - `src/app/lyric-vj/render/engine.ts` held settle (`if (on < fl + EXIT_LEAD ...) this.overlaps.push`), and
     `src/app/lyric-vj/render/line-shows.ts` (merges only consecutive lines).
- Two moment-line protections now coexist: engine.replaceLineBoxes (story-clear, sampled) and engine.keepClearOf (video-tier, 3-sample dry compose) in render/main.ts; consolidate into one (keep the sampled measure).
